Even the logs disagree

Under EIP-7708 a native USDC movement emits a Transfer at 18 decimals from a system address. The ERC-20 contract emits its own at 6.

One payment, two log streams, two scales. An indexer that treats them as one token either double-counts or is off by a trillion.
